# 🔍 Business Insights

## 1️⃣ Product Views are Extremely High Compared to Purchases

The majority of customer interactions were product views, while only a small percentage resulted in completed purchases.

📌 **Business Impact:**  
- Customers are browsing products but not converting efficiently.  
- Businesses should optimize product pages and checkout flow.  
- Better product recommendations and trust signals may improve conversions.  

---

## 2️⃣ Major Customer Drop-Off Occurs Between View and Cart Stage

The funnel analysis revealed that a significant number of users leave the platform after viewing products without adding them to the cart.

📌 **Business Impact:**  
- Product descriptions and visuals may not be engaging enough.  
- Businesses should improve CTA buttons and product presentation.  
- Retargeting ads can help recover potential customers.  

---

## 3️⃣ Cart Abandonment Affects Overall Conversion Performance

A noticeable percentage of users added products to the cart but did not complete purchases.

📌 **Business Impact:**  
- Checkout process may be too lengthy or complicated.  
- Businesses can introduce:
  - Faster checkout
  - Discount coupons
  - Free shipping offers
  - Reminder notifications
- Reducing cart abandonment can directly increase revenue.  

---

## 4️⃣ Certain Product Categories Dominate Customer Engagement

Some product categories consistently generated higher views, cart additions, and purchases compared to others.

📌 **Business Impact:**  
- High-performing categories should receive more marketing investment.  
- Businesses can introduce similar products within successful categories.  
- Inventory planning should prioritize top-performing categories.  

---

## 5️⃣ Mid-Range Products Receive Higher Engagement

Products within the medium price range showed better customer interaction and purchase activity compared to extremely high-priced products.

📌 **Business Impact:**  
- Customers prefer affordable products with balanced value.  
- Pricing strategies should focus on competitive mid-range pricing.  
- Businesses can use bundle offers to improve conversion further.  

---

## 6️⃣ Peak Purchase Activity Occurs During Specific Hours

The hourly purchase trend analysis showed that customer purchasing activity increases during certain hours of the day.

📌 **Business Impact:**  
- Businesses should run advertisements during peak activity hours.  
- Flash sales and promotional notifications can improve conversions.  
- Marketing campaigns should be scheduled according to customer activity trends.  

---

## 7️⃣ Some Brands Generate Significantly Higher Interaction Rates

Certain brands consistently received higher customer engagement and purchase activity.

📌 **Business Impact:**  
- Top-performing brands should be prioritized in promotions.  
- Businesses can collaborate more with high-performing brands.  
- Similar product recommendations can increase cross-selling opportunities.  

---

## 8️⃣ Daily User Activity Shows Consistent Customer Engagement

The platform maintained stable daily user engagement with fluctuations during high-traffic periods.

📌 **Business Impact:**  
- Consistent engagement indicates strong customer interest.  
- Businesses should maintain regular campaigns and product updates.  
- High-traffic periods can be targeted for special promotional events.
